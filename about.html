Our story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood gathering place built around fresh baking and friendly service. We make familiar favorites in small batches and enjoy helping customers choose something for breakfast, a family meal, or a celebration.

Thoughtful ingredients

We aim to source ingredients from nearby suppliers when they are available and in season. We share ingredient information when customers ask, and we encourage anyone with an allergy to contact us before placing an order.

The people behind the counter

Our bakers prepare the dough and pastries each morning, while our front-of-house team helps neighbors with selections and preorder questions. We work together to make every visit welcoming.
